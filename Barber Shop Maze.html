For grown-ups

Read The Haircut together first, then play. Your child walks with the arrows. At each gold gate they listen to the word and tap the matching card. A wrong tap costs nothing: the word is read again.

After each level, point to the sentence and read it together. Then hunt for the same words in the book: the, I, to, go and the rest. Children can come back as often as they like, and the words they have found stay lit.

If there is no sound on your device, read each word aloud yourself. The word cards work either way. Words practised: I, am, a, is, to, go, no, the, in, me, my, it.
